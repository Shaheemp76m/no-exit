## Initial design entry
### Design discovery — The Silent Place
- **Goal:** Establish the approved high-level direction before implementation.
- **Confirmed concept:** First-person existential psychological horror in a dreamlike liminal world that changes around a protagonist who has been there before.
- **Core sensory direction:** Deep, uncomfortable silence; dreamlike liminal visuals; environmental changes tied to memory; unreliable narration used sparingly.
- **Prototype:** The Silent Place — a small, playable atmospheric slice with an authored environmental transformation and ambiguous reveal.
- **Scope guardrail:** Prototype first. No need for enemies, combat, full procedural generation, or multiple endings in the first milestone.
- **Test results:** None claimed here. Repository inspection and implementation tests must be recorded when actually performed.
