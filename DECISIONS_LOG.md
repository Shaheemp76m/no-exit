## Initial owner-approved decisions

### DEC-001 — First-person existential psychological horror
- **Status:** Owner-approved
- **Decision:** No Exit is a first-person existential psychological horror game.
- **Reason:** Confirmed in the 20-question design interview.

### DEC-002 — Liminal world and memory-driven change
- **Status:** Owner-approved
- **Decision:** The setting is a mixture of liminal environments, and returning spaces change in response to the protagonist's memories.
- **Reason:** Confirmed in the design interview.

### DEC-003 — Environment as the primary threat
- **Status:** Owner-approved
- **Decision:** The environment itself is the threat; conventional enemies are not required for the first prototype.
- **Reason:** Confirmed in the design interview.

### DEC-004 — Silence and dreamlike visual direction
- **Status:** Owner-approved
- **Decision:** Deep, uncomfortable silence and a dreamlike liminal aesthetic are the primary sensory direction.
- **Reason:** Confirmed in the design interview.

### DEC-005 — Experimental prototype first
- **Status:** Owner-approved
- **Decision:** Build a small prototype before committing to full-game scope.
- **Reason:** Confirmed in the design interview.

### DEC-006 — The Silent Place
- **Status:** Owner-approved
- **Decision:** The first prototype focuses on liminal atmosphere, with a small environment and at least one authored environmental transformation.
- **Reason:** Confirmed in the design interview and approved summary.

### DEC-007 — Preserve mystery
- **Status:** Owner-approved at high level
- **Decision:** The protagonist has been here before but does not initially know why; do not define the full backstory or final truth in the first prototype.
- **Reason:** Preserves the approved concept of memory-driven existential dread.
